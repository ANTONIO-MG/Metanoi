# Week 1 · Topic 1 Challenge — Conduct & Legislation

## Scenario

You are the audit manager at MD&Co, the external auditor of **Ubuntu Mobile Ltd**, a public
interest entity. Mr Marais, the engagement partner, holds Ubuntu shares through his retirement
annuity. Fees from Ubuntu have been 18% of MD&Co's income for three consecutive years. Ubuntu's CFO,
a CA(SA), has capitalised R40 million of operating expenses to inflate profit and told the team to
"keep it off the audit's radar".

## Question (a) — 7 marks

### Required

Discuss the threats to MD&Co's independence arising from the partner's shareholding and the fee
level, in terms of the SAICA Code of Professional Conduct.

### Marking guide

| # | Answer point | Authority | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The shareholding is an indirect financial interest creating a self-interest threat. | SAICA CPC s510 | 2 |
| 2 | For a PIE engagement partner a material indirect interest is prohibited — the shares must be disposed of or the partner removed. | SAICA CPC s510 | 1 |
| 3 | Fees above 15% of firm income for consecutive years create a self-interest / intimidation threat. | SAICA CPC s410 | 2 |
| 4 | For a PIE the firm must disclose this to those charged with governance and arrange a pre- or post-issuance review. | SAICA CPC s410 | 2 |

## Question (b) — 5 marks

### Required

Explain whether the CFO's conduct is a reportable irregularity and set out MD&Co's reporting duties.

### Marking guide

| # | Answer point | Authority | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unlawful act: deliberate capitalisation of opex is fraudulent financial reporting. | APA s1 | 1 |
| 2 | By a person responsible for management, causing material loss to users. | APA s1 | 1 |
| 3 | Report to the IRBA without delay, in writing. | APA s45(1) | 1 |
| 4 | Notify the board in writing within 3 days and allow representations. | APA s45(2) | 1 |
| 5 | Second report to the IRBA within 30 days of the first. | APA s45(3) | 1 |

## Question (c) — 1 mark — MCQ

### Required

Which threat does the CFO's instruction to "keep it off the audit's radar" create for the audit team?

### Options

- [ ] Self-review
- [ ] Advocacy
- [x] Intimidation
- [ ] Familiarity

### Explanation

Pressure from management to conceal a matter from the auditor is an intimidation threat to objectivity.
