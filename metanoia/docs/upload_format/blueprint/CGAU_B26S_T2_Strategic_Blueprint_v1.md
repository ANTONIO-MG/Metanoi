# CGAU B26S Test 2 — Strategic Blueprint

Test 2: **Thursday 8 October 2026**, 100 marks, 150 minutes writing + 30 minutes reading.

## What you need to know before Test 2

Test 2 moves from planning into the evidence half of the audit: substantive procedures, the
Companies Act provisions an auditor tests, governance and the audit opinion.

## 3. Marker watch list — top avoidable errors

1. Substantive procedures written as tests of controls.
2. Citing "ISA 540" without saying what it requires of this estimate.
3. Concluding "material" without comparing to materiality.
