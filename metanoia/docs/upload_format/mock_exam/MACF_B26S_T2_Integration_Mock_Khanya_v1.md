# MACF B26S Test 2 — Integration Mock (Khanya Power Solutions)

## Scenario

Khanya Power Solutions Ltd designs, assembles and installs lithium battery and inverter systems. It is
listed on the JSE with a 28 February year-end. The corporate tax rate is 27%. The board is considering
a commercial inverter line requiring R123 000 000.

## Appendix A — market data

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Risk-free rate | 9.40% |
| Market return | 15.60% |
| Khanya equity beta | 1.15 |

## Question (a) — 10 marks — COMPUTATION

### Required

Calculate Khanya's cost of equity using the CAPM.

### Marking guide (computation)

| Line | Authority | Amount | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market risk premium (%) | CAPM | 6.20 | 3 |
| Beta × premium (%) | CAPM | 7.13 | 3 |
| Cost of equity (%) | CAPM | 16.53 | 4 |

### Required

Advise the board how the R123 000 000 should be funded.
