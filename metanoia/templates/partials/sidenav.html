{% load people %}{% load static %} {% comment %}Sidebar navigation. Styled by css/sidenav.css to match the MyHub dashboard: Inter, the dashboard's ink black for labels and a single blue for every icon and the active state — no per-item colour rainbow. Icons are the same Bootstrap Icons the dashboard cards use, so a section reads the same in both places. Sections are role-scoped by the flags from core.roles (via the user_roles context processor). Educator and admin sections group their links into collapsible sub-menus so a long teaching list stays scannable. Profile card: the drawer brand is the Metanoia logo (it was the old theme's "EDUMIN" wordmark), with the website's hero in miniature behind the profile picture. Your profile is linked once here (the name) and once in the navbar's avatar menu — not from the picture, a card footer and the footer links as well. A candidate gets three study tiles — the numbers they should be able to answer at a glance: what they are carrying, how far through the year's tests and exams they are, and whether that is enough for the one coming (built by ``apps.accounts.context_processors._study_tiles``). They are a candidate's numbers, so the admin team and educators (who have no modules or tests of their own) do not get them. A parent has no studies of their own, so the card names who they follow instead. Sections, in order: - Common nav (everyone): skipped entirely for parents — every item either has no meaning for them (the programme feed) or is repeated in their own section, arranged around their child rather than around a learner's own work. Dashboard is not listed: it lives in the navbar beside My Programme, where it is reachable from every page including the ones with no sidebar. - Metanoia Community member: everything a member can reach — ``CommunityAccessMiddleware`` enforces it; this menu is the convenience, not the control. - Metanoia Mail (``apps.mailbox``) — admin, staff and educators. The badge is the unread count across their inboxes; ``chrome.js`` keeps it live. - Student / member: no "My Programme" (the navbar carries it on every page, and the modules it lists are this "My Modules"). Online sessions replace the old "Courses" library — what a candidate attends is a live coaching session, not a self-paced course — and are one entry: the page itself switches between Upcoming and Past (``livesessions/_sessions_switch.html``). No Assessments, Revision or My Tasks: the owner took them out of the student hub, and ``core.student_hidden`` sends a student who opens those pages directly back to the dashboard. - Parent / guardian: a parent's nav is the whole of their platform — their child's progress, the money, and a line to the school. Everything listed is also enforced server-side by ``ParentAccessMiddleware``; this menu is the convenience, not the control. Announcements from the school arrive as notifications — the announcements page itself is the sender's view. - Educator, grouped into collapsible sub-menus: Content (what the educator authors and delivers), Assessment (setting, marking and closing the loop), My classes (the day-to-day running of a class) and Insights (how the class and its students are doing — grades only; certificates are not an educator's to browse). - Admin / staff: one Moderation page (chat flags, penalties, appeals, reputation and community posts, as tabs). The legacy theme groups (Educators / Students / Staff / Library / Holidays / Fees) were removed: their pages were never built in Metanoia and every link 404'd. People are managed under "Members & enrolment", money under Operations → Finance. Courses are gone from Metanoia: what a candidate registers for is an institution's programme and its modules, created and edited under Learning Hub → Academic structure. The staff desk holds the jobs that used to need Django admin or a shell. Developer tools come from ``apps.accounts.developers``; the error log and code dictionary are for developers only (every diagnostics view 404s for anyone else, administrators included). No Assessments / Reports in this group (owner's request); building and marking stay under Learning Hub. - All Content indexes the whole platform, so it is for everyone except a parent — whose hub is their child, not the institution. - Django admin is for admins only. ``/admin/`` never asks for a second login — the hub session IS the admin session (see ``config.auth_urls``). The closing script marks the link matching the current path as active and opens the sub-menu that contains it, so a deep page shows where it lives. The longest match wins, so ``/myhub/all-courses/`` beats ``/myhub/``.{% endcomment %}
