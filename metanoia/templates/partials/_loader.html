{% comment %}Global loading indicator — a slim top progress bar + a centre spinner overlay. Shows automatically on same-origin navigation and form submits, and is controllable from JS via window.appLoader.show() / .hide() (e.g. around fetch()). Link clicks are skipped for new tabs, downloads, modifier-clicks, ``#`` / ``javascript:`` / ``mailto:`` / ``tel:`` links, Bootstrap toggles (tabs, dropdowns, modals) and external hosts; a form marked ``data-no-loader`` (e.g. search-as-you-type) is skipped too. The bar hides on ``pageshow`` / ``DOMContentLoaded``, which also covers bfcache restores.{% endcomment %}
