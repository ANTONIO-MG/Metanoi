Registration sends exactly two e-mails and they do not overlap: 1. **this one** — thank you, what you registered for, and how to get going; 2. registration-document.html — the invoice or the receipt, as a PDF. So there is deliberately no money anywhere on this page: no total, no balance, no invoice number, no due date, no pay button. A reader who wants the figures opens the attachment on the other e-mail, which is the document they can file or forward to a sponsor. Splitting it that way is the point — the previous version put the total on this e-mail *and* attached the PDF *and* let a third "invoice" e-mail go out beside it, so three messages arrived saying overlapping things. There is also no personal detail: no e-mail address, no phone number, no physical address, no ID number. The recipient knows who they are, and an e-mail that recites their contact details is a small leak to anyone reading over their shoulder or to whoever they forward the thread to.
