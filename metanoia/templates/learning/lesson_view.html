Blocks render through learning/_lesson_blocks.html; behaviour lives in static/js/lesson-player.js. The lesson body renders in author order; section blocks become collapsible panels, followed by any YouTube session recordings a staff member attached. The rail has Info (with live presence — who else is in the lesson right now — and hand-raising over the WebSocket), Notes, Bookmarks, Scores and Messages tabs. Page styles cover the key-point boxes in imported study guides and blueprints (core.metanoia_materials.textconv), the presence roster and hand-raising. Inline scripts: - ``window.LESSON_PLAYER`` configures lesson-player.js; its ``notes`` map is ``{"
": ""}`` plus ``""`` for the whole-lesson note. - Article reading (only when ``article`` is set): a part counts as read once its end scrolls into view, and the contents list follows the heading being read. It posts to the same endpoint as the player's "Mark complete", so progress, the ring and "Finish lesson" behave the same. - Live presence and hand-raising: a peer-announced roster (``id -> {id, name, avatar, hand}``) of who is viewing the lesson right now over ``ws/lesson//``, plus a raise / lower-hand toggle every viewer sees; the toggle updates the roster optimistically before the socket echoes it.
